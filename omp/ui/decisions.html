Keyboard shortcuts ?

From omp, Ctrl Alt I controls the inbox from the prompt. q returns to omp.

j k move between questions. h l move between choices. Space selects a choice, or press its number.

i writes a reply. Esc keeps your draft. In the omp editor, Ctrl G cancels the edit. x clears the current reply.

Enter sends the current reply. Shift Enter sends all selected replies.
